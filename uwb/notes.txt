use USB-C port of the BU03 kit module and connect to USB-A port of Pi. If you are using a fresh pi, then most likely, you will not be able to detect the port /dev/ttyACM0. this is most likely because of a conflict with a background service called brltty which is a driver for braille displays for visually impaired users. to solve this issue, simple run "sudo apt remove brltty" and replug the USB cable and you should be good.
